## The four learning outcomes

PL is Personal Leadership. AA is Analysis and Advice. OR is Design and Realisation. MS is Management and Collaboration.

## What everyone does, whatever their profile

Four things do not depend on profile or level.

1. **Sprint review.** One per sprint, sprints 1 to 4. Outcome AA, OR, or both. Feedback from the team coach to the whole project team.
2. **Retro and updated PDP.** One per sprint, sprints 1 to 4. Outcome PL. Feedback from the team coach to you alone.
3. **Knowledge test.** Sprint 2 and sprint 4. No single learning outcome is named for it.
4. **Profile specific datapoints.** Two per sprint, sprints 1 to 4. These are the ones that differ per person, and they are set out below.

That makes one datapoint in sprint 0, four in sprint 1, five in sprint 2, four in sprint 3 and five in sprint 4.

## How to find your own evidence on the board

Filter the board on your outcome label and your level label together, rather than rebuilding the list from memory at the review. The labels in use are `outcome::analysing-and-advising`, `outcome::designing-and-realising`, `outcome::managing-and-collaborating`, `level::2` and `level::3`. Name the outcome and the level in the merge request description too, so the link from code to outcome survives after the issue closes.
