# Task 9: compact issue and task templates

Relates to #9. Second merge request for this task, after the merge request template.

## What changed

The team said in the retro of sprint 1 that issues and tasks were too long. An open issue had 2257 words on average and a task 2301. The rules in docs/team/issueRules.md and the templates in .gitlab/issue_templates now ask for a short card: a few plain sentences, a happy path with numbered unhappy paths for developers, acceptance criteria in plain words, and the Definition of done in short. A task is the story, What, a Checklist and a short BoKSA block, without acceptance criteria and without Definition of done. Issue #25 went from 3025 to 722 words and its task #320 from 3037 to 416.
