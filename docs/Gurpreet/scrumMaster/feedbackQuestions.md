## The questions

One question per criterion of level 3, written so the answer is an example and not only a yes or a no.

| Code | Criterion, level 3 | Question |
|---|---|---|
| MS1 | You anticipate risks and bottlenecks in the process before they become problems and ensure that the work remains transparent and transferable. | Name one moment this sprint where I saw a problem coming before it stopped you or the team. Was the board clear enough that you could take over someone else's task? What did you miss? |
| MS2 | You critically evaluate progress and cooperation, recognize patterns and proactively adjust the process where necessary. | When I compared the progress with the Sprint Goal, did that change anything in how you worked? Which change helped, and which one did not? |
| MS3 | You address team members constructively about behavior and contribution, even in complex or tense situations. | When I spoke to you or to the team about an agreement or a contribution, how did that feel, and did it lead to something? What should I do differently next time? |
| MS4 | You ensure that your work connects with that of others and contributes to the joint result. | Did the daily log and the links on the board help you see how your work fits with the work of the others? Give an example where it did, or where it did not. |
| MS5 | You support others in their contribution to the team goal. | When you were stuck, did I help you take the next step yourself, without taking over your work? What did I do that helped, and what would have helped more? |
| General | | What is the one thing I should keep doing as Scrum Master, and the one thing I should stop or change? |

For Bayon Eerhart also: did the split between Product Owner (priorities and the client) and Scrum Master (how the team works) stay clear in this sprint?
