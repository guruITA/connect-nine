# Agreement with the Product Owner, 19 October 2026

Gurpreet Singh (Scrum Master) and Bayon Eerhart (Product Owner) settle on the first day of sprint 2 who does what. Task: [#1485][1485].

The collaboration agreement, section 5: the Scrum Master organizes the daily stand-up, keeps the impediment log and removes impediments. The Product Owner keeps the contact with the client and other stakeholders, and keeps the quality of the project.

## Points to discuss

| # | Point | Proposal | Agreed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who does what | Product Owner: priorities, the order of the board and the client. Scrum Master: how the team works, the stand-up, the log and removing impediments | Agreed: the Scrum Master organizes the daily stand-up, keeps the impediment log and removes impediments (collaboration agreement, section 5). The Product Owner sets the priorities and the order of the board, keeps the contact with the client and other stakeholders, and keeps the quality of the project. Questions for the client go through the Product Owner (point 4) |
| 2 | The Sprint Goal | "A person can make an account, log in and have a profile, and the team knows which personal data it must protect" is in the milestone and is not changed during the sprint | Settled: the milestone Sprint 2 holds this goal word for word (checked on 19 October), and docs/project/sprintGoals.md says the sprint goal is written before the sprint starts and is not changed during it |
| 3 | Work that does not fit | When the board check finds that the remaining work does not fit the time left, the Scrum Master reports it and the Product Owner decides what moves | Agreed: the Scrum Master reports it when the board check finds that the remaining work does not fit. What moves follows decision 004 (docs/decisions/004-replanAfterSprint1.md, accepted by the whole team on 9 October): about 60 weight per sprint, a must or school goal that does not fit goes to the sprint with the most room left and is never taken out of the plan, and the whole team plans sprints 3 to 5 again after the review of sprint 2 |
| 4 | Questions for the client | A team member who needs an answer from the client asks the Product Owner, not the client directly. The Scrum Master follows the waiting time in the log | Agreed at the stand-up of 19 October (daily log, A1): a team member sends the question to Bayon Eerhart, who writes it down and asks the client |
| 5 | The stand-up | When and how long: in person on the project days on campus, written on the other days, with the three points of section 5 | Settled: the stand-up is online on Discord at 9:00. Gurpreet Singh writes down everything about the work that is said in the daily log |
| 6 | The daily log | What it holds and why: facts about the work only, so problems are not forgotten and the board stays right | Settled: Gurpreet Singh told the whole team about the daily log in the WhatsApp group, and everyone knows it. The message is kept as proof for his datapoint |
| 7 | Sprint review and retrospective | Who prepares what for the review at the client and the retrospective on the last physical lesson day of the sprint | Settled: the whole team prepares the sprint review at the client and the retrospective together |
| 8 | Feedback | Bayon Eerhart answers the feedback questions in the week of 26 October | Agreed: Bayon Eerhart agrees. The feedback for the datapoint of Gurpreet Singh was discussed with the whole team in the WhatsApp group |
| 9 | Escalation | A problem the team cannot solve quickly goes to the team coach Karthik Srinivasan, as section 8 of the collaboration agreement says | Settled: the teacher already knows this way of working. Karthik Srinivasan cannot be there for now, so Rob Kiebert stands in. He said the team can always come to him when there is a problem or something is not clear |
