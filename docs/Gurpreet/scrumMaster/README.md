Gurpreet Singh is the Scrum Master of Connect Nine in sprint 2, which runs from 19 October to 6 November 2026 (docs/project/sprintGoals.md). The proof covers 19 October to 2 November, because issue [#1489][1489] has all proof in Portflow by 2 November for the assessment of MS1 to MS5 on 3 November. Bayon Eerhart is the Product Owner. This folder holds the proof for the custom datapoint on Managing and Collaborating, Full Stack Software Developer level 3 (MS1 to MS5).

Board issue: [#1483][1483]. Tasks: [#1484][1484] preparation, [#1485][1485] board check, [#1486][1486] daily log, [#1487][1487] progress against the Sprint Goal, [#1488][1488] two team problems, [#1489][1489] feedback and Portflow.

This is personal learning and proof. It builds nothing in Connect and changes no team agreement. Priorities and contact with the client stay with the Product Owner.

## Rules for the log

- Only facts about the work. No personal matters and no criticism of a person.
- A team member who works from home sends a written update with three things: what they did, what they will do, and any blockers (collaboration agreement, section 5). That update goes in the log of that day.
- Every problem gets an owner, a next step and a deadline in problemLog.md. Its status is updated in that one row for the whole sprint, until it is solved and the result is written.
